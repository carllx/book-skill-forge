# Karsten Harries - Art Matters: The Origin of the Work of Art (Part 3)

## Prerequisites & Context

**Why:** Modernity, as characterized by the "Age of the World Picture," has led to a disintegration of Western culture. By adopting a strictly Cartesian or metaphysical approach, we risk reducing the world to mere representation (a picture) and human beings to isolated, disembodied subjects (res cogitans). This workflow is designed to counteract this "unbearable lightness of being" and technological alienation by rooting creative and architectural work in authentic, grounded existence (the "earth").

**When:** Apply this workflow when designing systems, user experiences, or architectures that run the risk of over-objectifying the user or the environment. It is crucial when a project feels rootless, purely transactional, or disconnected from its cultural and existential foundations.

## Comprehensive Guide & Best Practices

### 1. Rooting the Design (Dwelling Near the Origin)
True creativity requires that we remain rooted in our native soil rather than floating as displaced persons in a globalized, overly abstract metropolis.
*   **Heuristic:** Build "buildings," not just "pictures." A picture can only be observed; a building can be dwelt within. 
*   **Actionable Step:** Ensure that your system or design provides physical or spiritual shelter. It must be an environment the user can inhabit, not just a dashboard or simulacrum they passively consume.
> *"Schwer verlässt / Was nahe dem Ursprung wohnet, den Ort." (Reluctantly / That which dwells near its origin departs.)* — Hölderlin

### 2. Resisting Metaphysical Reduction
Metaphysics historically equates reality with what we can fully comprehend and control, culminating in a technological worldview that treats everything (including human beings) as raw material.
*   **Heuristic:** Do not force every element of your design to be perfectly analyzable into simple, mechanistic components.
*   **Actionable Step:** Leave room for the unquantifiable. Recognize that understanding something deeply often surpasses mere "know-how" or technical mastery. Allow the "earth" (the unrevealed, natural ground) to assert itself against the "world" (the structured, illuminated system).

### 3. Embracing Art as a Primordial Leap (*Ur-sprung*)
Origin (*Ursprung*) is not just a chronological starting point; it is a primordial leap that lets something original spring forth.
*   **Heuristic:** Treat the creation of your work not merely as the execution of a set of requirements, but as a foundational act that establishes a new way of seeing.
*   **Actionable Step:** When initiating a new architectural phase, ask yourself: *What is the fundamental paradigm shift this project brings into the light?* The work must be the origin of the creator as much as the creator is the origin of the work.

## If/Then Troubleshooting Logic

*   **IF** the project feels disconnected, superficial, or "unbearably light," **THEN** you have likely adopted an overly aesthetic or pictorial approach. **ACTION:** Shift the paradigm from "World Picture" to "World Building." Reintroduce elements that require user commitment, dwelling, and grounded interaction.
*   **IF** users feel alienated, treated as ghostly subjects, or reduced to mere data points, **THEN** the design is suffering from Cartesian displacement (the subject falling out of the world). **ACTION:** Re-embed the user experience into a specific, localized context. Restore the dignity of the embodied user rather than treating them as a theoretical *res cogitans*.
*   **IF** the system becomes purely a machine for efficiency, breeding "machine worries" and "machine hearts," **THEN** the technological framework (*Ge-stell*) has overtaken the organic purpose. **ACTION:** Consult the `graphify_query` MCP tool regarding the "Age of the World Picture" and re-evaluate the core metrics—prioritize meaning and cultural rootedness over raw performance.

## Verification Checklists

- [ ] **Dwelling vs. Observing:** Does the design invite the user to *dwell* within it, rather than merely observe it from the outside?
- [ ] **Rootedness:** Are the cultural, historical, and contextual roots of the project clearly acknowledged and integrated?
- [ ] **Resistance to Objectification:** Have you verified that the architecture avoids reducing human participants to mere technological material or abstract data points?
- [ ] **The "Primordial Leap":** Does the current iteration of the work represent a genuine *Ursprung* (a meaningful leap) rather than a rote continuation of the status quo?
