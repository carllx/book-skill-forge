# Workflow: Analyzing Neoclassical Theatricality and Oath Pictures

## Purpose
To evaluate Neoclassical historical or mythological paintings, focusing on how dramatic gestures and austere compositions are used to convey moral clarity and civic duty.

## Heuristics
- **Theatrical Staging**: Treat the painting as a shallow stage set where action plays out parallel to the picture plane.
- **Interlocking Gestures**: Look for highly choreographed, almost mime-like physical interactions that communicate the narrative instantaneously.
- **Moral Clarity**: Identify the central virtue being extolled (e.g., patriotism, sacrifice, stoicism).

## Execution Steps
1. **Narrative Decoupling**: Identify the exact moment of the story being depicted. Why is this specific gesture or 'oath' the climax of the narrative?
2. **Spatial Analysis**: Examine the depth of the space. Note how the background is often flattened or uses architectural elements to push the figures forward.
3. **Gesture Mapping**: Trace the lines of sight and the physical connections between the characters\' hands and arms. How do these lines direct the viewer\'s attention to the focal point?
4. **Contextualization**: Connect the visual melodrama to the broader cultural or political climate of the time (e.g., the Enlightenment, the French Revolution).
