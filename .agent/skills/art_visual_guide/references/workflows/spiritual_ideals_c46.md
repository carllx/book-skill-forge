# Workflow: Spiritual ideals

## Purpose
To analyze early abstract art through the lens of their shared goal to oppose material values with profound spiritual ideals.

## Heuristics
- **Opposition to Materialism**: Does the artwork actively reject or challenge self-limiting material values?
- **Leap into the Future**: Is there an attempt to "turn back the clock" or leap into a new utopian future?
- **Abstract Vocabulary**: How does the artist's specific abstract style serve their spiritual objectives?

## Steps
1. **Identify the Abstract Style**: Categorize the specific abstract approach used by the artist.
2. **Extract the Spiritual Intent**: Determine the spiritual or philosophical goals behind the work.
3. **Analyze the Rejection of Materialism**: Evaluate how the work formally or conceptually opposes material values.
4. **Assess the Vision**: Conclude how successfully the work articulates a new set of spiritual ideals.
