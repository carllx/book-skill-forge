# Workflow: Bronze

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow guides the analysis of Bronze sculpture, specifically evaluating the production techniques and their historical impact on reproducibility.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Analyze Material Properties**: Consider the implications of using an alloy of copper and tin (durability, weight, surface finish).
2. **Evaluate the Casting Process**: Understand the lost-wax casting technique and how the liquid metal interacts with the mould.
3. **Determine Reproducibility Constraints**: Identify whether the piece was created with a single-use (broken) mould or a piece mould (post-18th century).
4. **Contextualize Production Eras**: Frame the work historically based on the transition to piece-moulding for multiple editions.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce an assessment of the bronze sculpture that balances material science with artistic intention and historical production methods.
