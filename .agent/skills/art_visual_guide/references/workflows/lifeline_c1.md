# Workflow: Analyzing the Artist as Institution Builder

## Objective
To construct a chronological analysis of an artist's career (e.g., Charles Willson Peale) who transitions from a practicing painter to an institutional founder, shaping the cultural infrastructure of a new nation.

## Heuristics & Principles
1. **The Transatlantic Education:** Note the pattern of early artists seeking foundational training in established cultural capitals (London) before returning to build localized traditions.
2. **Art as Historical Record:** Recognize the strategic opening of galleries focused on contemporary heroes (e.g., Revolutionary figures) as a deliberate act of nation-building and self-promotion.
3. **The Pivot to Institutionalism:** Pay attention to the inflection point where the artist retires from active creation to focus entirely on preserving, exhibiting, and educating (founding a museum).

## Steps for Analysis
1. **Trace the Educational Arc:** Map the artist's origins to their international training and their subsequent return.
2. **Identify the Commercial/Cultural Pivot:** Note when the artist shifts from taking private commissions to establishing public or semi-public spaces (e.g., opening the first portrait gallery).
3. **Analyze the Institutional Legacy:** Examine the founding of major cultural institutions (e.g., the Peale Museum) and how it altered the local cultural landscape.
4. **Evaluate the Capstone Phase:** Analyze the final phase of the career where curation and institution management completely supersede individual artistic output.
