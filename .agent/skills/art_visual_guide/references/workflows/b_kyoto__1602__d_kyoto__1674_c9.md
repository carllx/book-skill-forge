# Workflow: b KYOTO, 1602; d KYOTO, 1674

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow guides the analysis of Kano Tan'yu and the broader influence of the Kano School as official painters to the Shogunate during the Edo period.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Understand Kano School Ideals**: Trace the lineage from Kano Eitoku to Kano Tan'yu and the expectations of official Shogunate painters.
2. **Analyze Official Commissions**: Evaluate the subject matter used to decorate castles and residences (e.g., authoritative symbols, historical grandeur).
3. **Assess Stylistic Synthesis**: Consider how Tan'yu balanced traditional Japanese ink brushwork with the grandeur required by Edo-period patrons.
4. **Identify Political Function**: Interpret the artwork as a reflection of political authority and societal ideals.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce an analysis that situates Kano Tan'yu's work within the political and cultural framework of the early Edo period.
