# Workflow: Complex Allegories (Philipp Otto Runge)

## 1. Core Concept
Constructing dense, multi-layered visual allegories that blend personal observation (like portraiture) with profound spiritual, mystical, or romantic cosmology.

## 2. Heuristics for Allegorical Construction
*   **Microcosm to Macrocosm:** Connect intimate, specific details (a specific plant, a child's face) to universal themes (times of day, seasons, life cycle).
*   **Symbolic Density:** Ensure every element in the composition serves a symbolic or metaphysical purpose.
*   **Color as Spirit:** Use color theory not just for naturalism, but as an expression of spiritual or emotional states.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Establish the Universal Theme:** Choose the overarching cosmological or philosophical idea (e.g., the cycle of dawn/morning).
2.  **Select Specific Motifs:** Choose grounded, realistic subjects (portraits of children, specific flora) to anchor the theme.
3.  **Weave the Symbology:** Arrange the motifs symmetrically or hierarchically to build the allegorical structure.
4.  **Apply Theoretical Frameworks:** Use rigorous theories (like Runge's color sphere) to dictate the formal execution of the work.
