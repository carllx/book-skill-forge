# Workflow: Analyzing Pointillism and Compositional Revision (Seurat)

## Purpose
To analyze Post-Impressionist works that rely on optical mixing (Pointillism) and rigorous compositional planning, using Georges Seurat\'s methodology as a guide.

## Heuristics
- **Optical Mixing**: Look for the juxtaposition of distinct dots or strokes of pure color intended to blend in the viewer\'s eye.
- **Static Geometry**: Identify the rigid, almost Egyptian profile-and-frontal poses that give figures an impersonal, monumental quality.
- **Iterative Refinement**: Recognize evidence of the artist reworking the canvas to achieve greater luminosity or structural harmony.

## Execution Steps
1. **Color Interaction Analysis**: Zoom in on the surface. How are contrasting colors placed side-by-side to create a specific hue or lighting effect?
2. **Structural Breakdown**: Map the vertical and horizontal axes of the composition. How do the figures and shadows lock into this grid?
3. **Character De-individualization**: Assess the figures. Note how the lack of facial details and rigid postures turn them into archetypes rather than individuals.
4. **Process Reconstruction**: If analyzing multiple states or studies, trace how the artist refined the composition from initial sketch to final, systematic execution.
