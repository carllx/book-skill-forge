# Workflow: Hidden Narratives and Social Commentary

## Purpose
To uncover hidden narratives (such as mortality) beneath joyful facades, and to analyze explicit social commentary in narrative art (e.g., Hogarth's works).

## Heuristics
- **Contrasting Symbols**: Do symbols of mortality or tragedy (e.g., scythe, hourglass) contrast with a seemingly cheerful facade (e.g., smiling children)?
- **Social Campaigning**: Was the artwork created as part of a campaign or to highlight a specific social issue (e.g., Gin Lane)?
- **Explicit vs. Implicit Meaning**: Are the warnings explicit (as in hellish visions of urban decay) or implicit (hidden in portraiture)?

## Steps
1. **Scan for Symbolic Objects**: Look closely for objects that might carry hidden meanings (e.g., clocks, hourglasses, animals).
2. **Analyze the Contrast**: Evaluate the tension between the overt mood (smiles, playfulness) and the underlying reality (death, loss).
3. **Identify Social Context**: Determine if the work addresses a contemporary social issue or vice (e.g., poverty, addiction).
4. **Synthesize the Message**: Conclude how the artist uses narrative and symbolism to deliver a moral or social message.
