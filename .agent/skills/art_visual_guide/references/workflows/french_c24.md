# Workflow: French Neoclassical Pedagogy & Influence (Jacques-Louis David)

## Objective
To trace the stylistic lineage and structural influence of a dominant artistic figure (like Jacques-Louis David) on the subsequent generation of artists within a specific movement (Neoclassicism).

## Heuristics & Principles
1. **The Artist as Archetype & Teacher:** Masterpieces establish the "rules" of a style, but teaching disseminates it. Evaluate an artist's impact not just by their own work, but by their pedagogical reach.
2. **Generational Continuity:** Look for the transmission of core neoclassical tenets (clarity, severe lines, moral subjects) to the master's pupils.
3. **Archetypal Mastery:** Identify the definitive works that serve as the template for an entire artistic movement.

## Steps for Analysis
1. **Identify the Archetype:** Pinpoint the defining masterpiece(s) of the movement (e.g., David's core neoclassical works).
2. **Trace the Pedagogical Tree:** Map the artist's students and followers. Who learned directly from them?
3. **Examine Stylistic Inheritance:** Analyze the pupils' works for inherited traits (e.g., rigid composition, thematic gravity).
4. **Assess Institutional Influence:** Determine how the artist's role as a teacher shaped the broader European art ecosystem of the era.
