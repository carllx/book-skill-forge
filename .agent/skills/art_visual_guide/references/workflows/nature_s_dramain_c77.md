# Workflow: Nature's Drama (Environmental Narrative)

## 1. Core Concept
Conveying the full impact of a narrative or emotional state through the natural setting and atmospheric effects rather than through human figures, as exemplified by J.M.W. Turner.

## 2. Heuristics for Atmospheric Storytelling
*   **Subordination of Figure:** Reduce human figures to secondary elements or mere scale indicators.
*   **Nature as Protagonist:** Use weather, light, and geological formations (e.g., clouds forming the shape of a cyclops) as the primary actors in the scene.
*   **Emotional Resonance:** Match the turbulence or serenity of the environment directly to the narrative's climax.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Define the Narrative Core:** What is the story or emotion to be conveyed (e.g., technological speed, mythical terror, sublime awe)?
2.  **Design the Atmosphere:** Select the environmental conditions (storm, fog, glaring sunlight) that embody this narrative.
3.  **Merge Form and Environment:** Dissolve boundaries between objects and weather (e.g., blending a monster into a cloud, or a train into rain).
4.  **Refine Lighting:** Use extreme contrasts or diffusing light to guide the viewer's emotional response.
