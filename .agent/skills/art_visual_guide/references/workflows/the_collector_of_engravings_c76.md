# Workflow: Realist Subjects

## Purpose
To analyze Realist artworks (like those of Daumier and Millet) that focus on unsentimental, keenly observed scenes of everyday life and the elevation of the working class.

## Heuristics
- **Unsentimental Observation**: Is the scene depicted with a keen, objective eye rather than romanticized sentimentality?
- **Elevation of the Ordinary**: Are ordinary subjects (e.g., peasants, urban dwellers) depicted as noble or heroic figures?
- **Social Reality**: Does the artwork highlight the harsh realities of labor or urban life (e.g., gleaning leftovers, city scenes)?

## Steps
1. **Identify the Subject**: Determine if the focus is on everyday people, laborers, or urban life.
2. **Analyze the Perspective**: Assess whether the artist's view is unsentimental and objectively observed.
3. **Evaluate Characterization**: Check if the subjects are imbued with a sense of nobility, heroism, or dignity despite their circumstances.
4. **Determine Social Commentary**: Conclude what the artwork reveals about the social realities of the time.
