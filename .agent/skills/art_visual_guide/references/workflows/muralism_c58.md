# Workflow: Muralism (Large-Scale Public Didactic Art)

## 1. Core Concept
Using monumental public spaces to convey complex political, historical, and social narratives to the masses, synthesizing diverse influences (e.g., Cubism, Renaissance frescoes, Marxism) into a cohesive monumental style, as seen in "Los Tres Grandes" (Orozco, Rivera, Siqueiros).

## 2. Heuristics for Monumental Synthesis
*   **Public Legibility:** The message must be readable by the general public, avoiding overly obscure personal symbolism.
*   **Architectural Integration:** The composition must respond to the scale and shape of the wall or public space.
*   **Ideological Clarity:** Whether utopian (Rivera) or darker and more critical (Orozco), the core ideological stance must drive the composition.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Site Analysis:** Assess the physical space, sightlines, and audience demographic.
2.  **Narrative Structuring:** Outline the historical or political story to be told (e.g., struggle, revolution, industry).
3.  **Stylistic Fusion:** Blend local/indigenous visual traditions with formal structural techniques (e.g., dynamic symmetry or modernist forms).
4.  **Scale Execution:** Project the design onto the large scale, ensuring the focal points guide the viewer's eye across the monumental surface.
