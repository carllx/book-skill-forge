# Workflow: bSAN GIOVANNI VALDARNO 1401; d ROME, 1428/29

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow directs the analysis of Masaccio's short but revolutionary career and his role as a foundational innovator of 15th-century Italian art.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Contrast with Contemporaries**: Differentiate his work from the prevailing International Gothic style, focusing on realism over ornamentation.
2. **Apply Perspective Laws**: Analyze his integration of Brunelleschi's mathematical laws of perspective to achieve flat-surface spatial illusion.
3. **Assess Sculptural Form**: Trace the influence of Donatello in the three-dimensional weight and volume of his painted figures.
4. **Evaluate Legacy**: Position his work as the critical bridge continuing the legacy of Giotto into the Early Renaissance.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce a structural critique of Masaccio's works highlighting the synthesis of mathematical perspective and sculptural form.
