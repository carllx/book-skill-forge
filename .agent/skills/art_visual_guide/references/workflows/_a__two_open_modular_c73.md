# Workflow: $A$ Two Open Modular

## Purpose
To evaluate artworks or systems based on Sol LeWitt's concept where the system and basic unit employed are more interesting than the units used to express the system.

## Heuristics
- **System over Expression**: Does the work prioritize the underlying logic and basic units over the specific materials used to express them?
- **Uninteresting Basics**: Are the basic units "relatively uninteresting" to force focus on the overall system?
- **Materiality**: Evaluate how the fabrication (e.g., enamelled aluminium) serves the idea rather than the artist's hand.

## Steps
1. **Identify the Core Unit**: Isolate the foundational element of the work (e.g., the open cube).
2. **Analyze the System**: Determine the logic or rules governing how the units are arranged or combined.
3. **Evaluate the Fabrication**: Assess whether the physical execution draws attention away from the system or reinforces it.
4. **Determine the Focus**: Conclude if the spectator is prompted to think about the system rather than the object itself.
