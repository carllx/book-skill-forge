# Workflow: Evaluating the Transition to Modernism (Édouard Manet)

## Purpose
To analyze works that bridge realism and modernism, focusing on the subversion of traditional techniques and the flattening of pictorial space, pioneered by Édouard Manet.

## Heuristics
- **Harsh Contrasts**: Look for the elimination of mid-tones (chiaroscuro), resulting in stark contrasts between light and dark.
- **Subverted Masterpieces**: Identify compositions that quote Old Master paintings but place them in jarring, contemporary contexts.
- **Acknowledgment of the Canvas**: Notice how the paint application draws attention to the flat surface of the canvas rather than attempting perfect illusionism.

## Execution Steps
1. **Tonal Analysis**: Evaluate the modeling of forms. Are the figures painted with sudden shifts from light to dark rather than smooth gradients?
2. **Source Identification**: Attempt to trace the composition back to a historical antecedent (e.g., Titian, Goya). How does the modern update change the meaning?
3. **Gaze and Confrontation**: Analyze the subject\'s interaction with the viewer. Does the figure look out with an impassive, challenging gaze that breaks the 'fourth wall'?
4. **Technical Assessment**: Look at the brushwork. Identify areas where the paint is applied broadly and flatly, prioritizing the materiality of the paint over realistic detail.
