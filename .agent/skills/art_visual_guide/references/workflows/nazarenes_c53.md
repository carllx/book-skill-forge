# Workflow: The Nazarenes & Romantic Revival

## 1. Core Concept
Reviving lost spiritual or national artistic purity by rejecting contemporary trends and returning to historical, religious, or early Renaissance models, often tinged with Romantic melancholy.

## 2. Heuristics for Revivalist Aesthetics
*   **Historical Anchoring:** Look past immediate predecessors to a "golden age" (e.g., early Italian Renaissance or medieval guilds).
*   **Sincere Spiritualism:** Prioritize earnest religious, moral, or national feeling over mere technical bravura.
*   **Melancholic Undertone:** Infuse the work with a sense of longing for a lost ideal (common in broader Romanticism, e.g., Constable's landscapes).

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Identify the Degeneration:** Determine what aspect of contemporary art/culture feels hollow or overly academic.
2.  **Select the Archetype:** Choose the historical era or style that represents purity and truth for the current project.
3.  **Adopt the Ethos:** Emulate not just the style, but the working methods or communal spirit (like a brotherhood or guild) of that era.
4.  **Inject Romantic Emotion:** Ensure the revival isn't sterile imitation, but is driven by deep personal or collective longing.
