# Workflow: Constructivist & Geometric Abstraction Analysis (El Lissitzky)

## Purpose
To analyze geometric abstraction and its application in early 20th-century avant-garde art, particularly focusing on the transition from figurative to non-objective forms.

## Heuristics
- **Dynamic Equilibrium**: Look for compositions that balance geometric elements (lines, squares, circles) in a state of suspended tension.
- **Spatial Illusion**: Identify how flat, geometric shapes are manipulated to suggest 3D architectural space (Prouns).
- **Utilitarian Aesthetics**: Consider how the visual language integrates engineering, architecture, and graphic design principles.

## Execution Steps
1. **Component Breakdown**: Catalog the primary geometric forms used in the composition.
2. **Spatial Dynamics**: Analyze the illusion of depth. Do the forms appear to advance or recede? Are there shifting axes or multiple perspectives?
3. **Ideological Context**: Relate the abstract forms to utopian or constructivist ideals. Does the artwork suggest a blueprint for a new society or architecture?
4. **Medium Intersection**: Evaluate how the piece blends the boundaries between painting, architecture, and graphic design.
