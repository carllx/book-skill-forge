# Workflow: Analyzing the Romantic Sublime (Caspar David Friedrich)

## Purpose
To analyze German Romantic landscape painting, focusing on how nature is used as a vehicle for spiritual contemplation and the sublime.

## Heuristics
- **The Rückenfigur**: Look for figures seen from behind, inviting the viewer to adopt their perspective and contemplate the vastness of nature.
- **Symbolic Landscapes**: Treat natural elements (ruins, dead trees, twilight, mist) not merely as scenery, but as symbols of mortality, the divine, or the infinite.
- **Atmospheric Melancholy**: Note the pervasive mood of stillness, introspection, or melancholy.

## Execution Steps
1. **Identify the Surrogate**: Locate the central figure(s). How does their positioning direct your gaze into the landscape?
2. **Symbolic Decoding**: Catalog the natural and architectural elements. What might the mist, the Gothic ruins, or the twisted oak represent allegorically?
3. **Scale and Proportion**: Compare the scale of the human figures to the vastness of the sky or mountains. How does this emphasize human insignificance in the face of nature?
4. **Emotional Synthesis**: Describe the specific spiritual or emotional resonance of the piece. Is it desolate, hopeful, or awe-inspiring?
