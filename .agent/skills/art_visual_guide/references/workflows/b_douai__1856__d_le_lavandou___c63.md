# Workflow: b DOUAI, 1856; d LE LAVANDOU, 1910

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow examines the stylistic evolution of Henri-Edmond Cross, from his early Realism to his pivotal role in Neo-Impressionism.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Track Stylistic Shifts**: Document the transition from his 1881 Salon Realism to his later, Monet-inspired Impressionism.
2. **Analyze Palette Changes**: Evaluate the deliberate lightening of his palette as he embraced pointillism and divisionism.
3. **Assess Institutional Impact**: Consider his influence as a co-founder of the Salon des Independants (1884).
4. **Synthesize Neo-Impressionist Role**: Connect his theoretical approach to color with the broader Neo-Impressionist movement alongside figures like Maximilien Luce.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce an analysis that traces Henri-Edmond Cross's trajectory and his technical contributions to color theory and the Salon des Independants.
