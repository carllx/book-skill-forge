# Workflow: Functional Art & Cultural Artifacts

## 1. Core Concept
Understanding and evaluating art whose primary genesis is function (e.g., navigation, settlement, ritual) rather than purely aesthetic display, common in rich indigenous traditions over thousands of years.

## 2. Heuristics for Functional Analysis
*   **Form Follows Function/Belief:** Analyze how the shape, decoration, or plainness directly serves a physical or spiritual utility.
*   **Material Continuity:** Observe how local materials dictate the techniques and limits of the artifact.
*   **Long-Term Cultural Transmission:** Recognize the artifact as part of a multi-generational knowledge system (like Polynesian navigation).

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Define the Utility:** What was this object used for (e.g., mapping ocean swells, religious ceremony)?
2.  **Analyze Materiality:** Document the materials and how they were sourced and worked.
3.  **Decode the Ornamentation:** Interpret the patterns or carvings not as mere decoration, but as encoded knowledge, genealogy, or spiritual protection.
4.  **Contextualize in History:** Place the object within the broader timeline of human settlement and cultural exchange.
