# Workflow: Analyzing the International Diffusion of Impressionism

## Objective
To track how a localized artistic movement (Impressionism in Paris) disrupts traditional academic rules and diffuses internationally through traveling artists who adapt the style to their home countries.

## Heuristics & Principles
1. **The Disruption of Rules:** Identify the core rule being broken—in this case, the "casual attitude to the laws of perspective"—which serves as the catalyst for rethinking traditional approaches.
2. **The Epicenter Effect:** Recognize the gravitational pull of a cultural capital (Paris) that attracts ambitious, international talent seeking the "latest trends."
3. **Global Translation:** Observe how foreign artists (e.g., Steer, Liebermann, Corinth, Hassam) absorb the epicenter's innovations and translate them into their native contexts.

## Steps for Analysis
1. **Identify the Core Disruption:** Pinpoint the specific academic rule that the movement is rejecting (e.g., strict perspective, formal underpainting).
2. **Map the Epicenter:** Establish the geographic and cultural focal point of the innovation.
3. **Trace the Expatriate Network:** Identify the international artists who traveled to the epicenter.
4. **Analyze the Domestic Adaptation:** Examine how these artists applied the new techniques (e.g., broken brushstrokes, plein air) upon returning to their home countries.
