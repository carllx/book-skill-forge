# Workflow: Style and Subjects

## Purpose
To evaluate Pop Art (and similar movements) based on its stylistic detachment and reliance on commercial imagery and mass production aesthetics.

## Heuristics
- **Rejection of the Painterly**: Does the work deliberately avoid expressive, painterly techniques in favor of flat colors and clean lines?
- **Commercial Aesthetics**: Is the imagery drawn from commercial art, advertising, or mass production?
- **Tone of Detachment**: Does the work feel detached, objective, or ironic rather than expressive of individual emotion?

## Steps
1. **Analyze the Technique**: Look for clean lines, hard edges, and flat color application.
2. **Identify the Subject Matter**: Determine if the imagery represents mundane, everyday life or commercial products.
3. **Evaluate the Tone**: Assess whether the presentation of the subject is ironic, detached, or objective.
4. **Determine the Implication**: Conclude how the style subverts traditional notions of artistic individuality in favor of mass production aesthetics.
