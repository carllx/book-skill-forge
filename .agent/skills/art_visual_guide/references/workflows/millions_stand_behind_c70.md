# Workflow: Millions Stand Behind (Subversive Political Art)

## 1. Core Concept
Subverting dominant political narratives or propaganda by subtly altering its elements to expose hidden truths (e.g., John Heartfield exposing Hitler's financial backing from industrialists).

## 2. Heuristics for Subversive Synthesis
*   **Identify the Target Message:** Pinpoint the exact slogan, imagery, or claim used by the target (e.g., "Millions stand behind me").
*   **Locate the Hidden Reality:** Identify the opposing truth or hidden mechanism (e.g., "Millions of *money*, not people").
*   **Visual Subversion:** Alter the visual context slightly so the original meaning is twisted into the new reality without losing the recognizable form of the original propaganda.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Analyze the Propaganda:** Document the original message and its intended psychological effect.
2.  **Determine the Counter-Narrative:** What is the actual socio-economic or political reality?
3.  **Synthesize the Alteration:** Introduce a visual or textual element that turns the propaganda upon itself (e.g., a hand offering money behind a saluting figure).
4.  **Evaluate Impact:** Ensure the subversion is immediately legible to the audience.
