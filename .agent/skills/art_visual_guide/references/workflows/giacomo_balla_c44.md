# Workflow: Analyzing Miniature Durable Artifacts (Cylinder Seals)

## Objective
To examine miniature, highly durable sculptural artifacts (like Akkadian cylinder seals) and understand their role as pervasive cultural and administrative tools in ancient societies.

## Heuristics & Principles
1. **Durability as Archival Evidence:** Recognize that the most durable mediums (e.g., stone or baked clay) often provide the most continuous historical record.
2. **Miniature Ubiquity:** Small, everyday items (like cylinder seals) offer critical insights into daily life, administration, and personal identity that monumental art may miss.
3. **Function Dictates Form:** Understand that the intricate carving on a cylinder seal is designed in reverse to create a continuous, repeating frieze when rolled onto wet clay.

## Steps for Analysis
1. **Identify the Medium and Scale:** Note the material's durability and the miniature scale of the artifact.
2. **Analyze the Functional Mechanism:** Determine how the object was used (e.g., rolled out to secure a document or establish identity).
3. **Examine the Iconography:** Read the visual narrative or symbols carved into the seal, paying attention to specific cultural markers (e.g., Akkadian stylistic traits).
4. **Assess Societal Pervasiveness:** Map how widespread the artifact's use was across the region (e.g., western Asia) to understand its administrative or economic importance.
