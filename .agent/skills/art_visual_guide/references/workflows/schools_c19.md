# Workflow: Schools

## Purpose
To analyze artistic schools and movements, particularly focusing on shifts in geographical centers of innovation (e.g., Neoclassicism from Rome to France) and the trajectory of individual prodigies within these schools.

## Heuristics
- **Geographical Shift**: Is the perceived center of an art movement the actual site of its most significant developments?
- **Individual vs. Movement**: How does a leading figure relate to the broader school they are associated with?

## Steps
1. **Identify the Artistic School**: Define the movement or school being analyzed.
2. **Locate the Center of Innovation**: Map the geographical origins versus the places of significant development.
3. **Analyze Key Figures**: Trace the development of prominent artists within the school.
4. **Evaluate Contributions**: Assess how the individual's work deviates from or reinforces the school's core tenets.
