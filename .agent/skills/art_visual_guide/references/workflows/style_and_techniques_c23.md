# Workflow: Style and Techniques

## Purpose
To analyze works within the Rococo style (or similar decorative periods), focusing on their light-hearted narrative approaches and ornamental virtuosity.

## Heuristics
- **Decorative Amplification**: Are narrative elements amplified by playful additions like putti or animated statues?
- **Virtuoso Execution**: Does the artist indulge in effortless, highly skilled, and ornamental flourishes?
- **Tone Mitigation**: Even in sombre themes, is the overall effect softened by a focus on decoration and light-heartedness?

## Steps
1. **Assess the Tone**: Determine if the overall mood of the artwork is light-hearted, playful, or highly decorative.
2. **Identify Decorative Elements**: Look for specific ornamental additions such as scrollwork, shell motifs, or putti.
3. **Analyze the Execution**: Evaluate the artist's technique for virtuoso effects and ornamental flourishes.
4. **Evaluate Narrative Impact**: Consider how the decorative elements alter or amplify the underlying narrative or theme.
