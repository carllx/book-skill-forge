# Workflow: Analyzing Pre-Impressionist Landscapes (Camille Corot)

## Purpose
To evaluate landscapes that bridge the gap between Neoclassical tradition and Impressionist spontaneity, using the work of Camille Corot as a benchmark.

## Heuristics
- **Tonal Harmony**: Look for a limited, unified color palette, often relying on silvery greens, soft grays, and muted browns.
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Prioritize the rendering of light, mist, and atmospheric conditions over sharp, topographical details.
- **Plein Air Influence**: Identify elements of direct observation from nature, balanced with studio composition.

## Execution Steps
1. **Atmosphere Assessment**: Evaluate the overall mood. How does the artist use tone and light to create a specific atmospheric quality?
2. **Structural Composition**: Check the underlying geometry. Despite soft brushwork, is there a solid classical structure (e.g., framing trees, distant horizons)?
3. **Brushwork Analysis**: Examine the application of paint. Note areas where the brushwork is loose and suggestive versus areas of tighter control.
4. **Historical Synthesis**: Place the work on the continuum between academic landscape painting and the fully realized optical approach of Impressionism.
