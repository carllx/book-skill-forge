# Workflow: b MADRID 1887; d BOULOGNE-BILLANCOURT, 1927

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow focuses on Juan Gris, exploring his specific contributions to the Cubist movement as its "third man" alongside Picasso and Braque.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Identify Stylistic Rigor**: Analyze Gris's structured, almost mathematical approach to geometric forms in his compositions.
2. **Evaluate Spatial Relationships**: Focus on how he clarifies the space and boundaries between overlapping objects.
3. **Examine Color Usage**: Contrast his distinct, vibrant color palette against the often monochromatic works of early Analytic Cubism.
4. **Consider Illustrative Background**: Assess how his early career as an illustrator informed his precise and readable Cubist aesthetic.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce a critique of Juan Gris's work that emphasizes his clarity, structural rigor, and unique color application within Cubism.
