# Workflow: Monumental Environmental Art

## Purpose
To evaluate monumental, temporary environmental artworks that transform public landmarks and engage with historical context.

## Heuristics
- **Transformation of the Familiar**: Does the artwork obscure or alter a well-known landmark to force a re-evaluation of its form and meaning?
- **Temporality**: How does the temporary nature of the work enhance its impact and urgency?
- **Historical Engagement**: Does the timing or location of the work intersect with significant historical events?

## Steps
1. **Identify the Subject**: Determine the landmark or environment being transformed.
2. **Analyze the Transformation**: Evaluate how the materials alter the object into a new abstraction.
3. **Assess the Context**: Consider the historical, political, and social significance of the site and the timing of the project.
4. **Evaluate the Experience**: Conclude how the temporary nature and monumental scale create a unique, enigmatic experience for the public.
