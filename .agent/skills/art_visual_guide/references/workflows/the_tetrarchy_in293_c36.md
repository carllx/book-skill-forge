# Workflow: Political Unity in Art

## Purpose
To evaluate artworks (like *The Four Tetrarchs*) created to project political unity, stability, and collective power over individual identity.

## Heuristics
- **Suppression of Individuality**: Are the figures depicted identically or similarly to emphasize a role over personal identity?
- **Projection of Unity**: Does the composition physically bind the figures together (e.g., embracing, identical poses) to suggest political solidarity?
- **Materiality and Permanence**: Is the material (e.g., porphyry) chosen for its durability and association with imperial power?

## Steps
1. **Analyze Figure Depiction**: Examine if the subjects are individualized or generalized to represent a collective concept.
2. **Evaluate Composition**: Look for physical gestures (like embraces or shared stances) that denote unity and shared responsibility.
3. **Assess the Medium**: Consider the symbolic weight and permanence of the material used.
4. **Determine the Political Message**: Conclude how the artwork functions as propaganda for political stability.
