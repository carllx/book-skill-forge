# Workflow: Artistic Paradigm Shifts & Obsolescence

## 1. Core Concept
Analyzing the lifecycle of an artist's career or a specific style, particularly focusing on the transition from peak success to obscurity due to sudden paradigm shifts in aesthetic taste (e.g., Botticelli eclipsed by the High Renaissance).

## 2. Heuristics for Paradigm Shift Analysis
*   **Identify the Peak:** Define the stylistic hallmarks that made the artist highly successful in their prime.
*   **Pinpoint the Shift:** Identify the new generation or movement (e.g., Leonardo da Vinci) that introduced fundamentally different values or techniques (e.g., sfumato, anatomical rigor).
*   **Analyze the Disconnect:** Determine why the older style suddenly appeared "outmoded" rather than just different.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Map the Artist's Trajectory:** Outline the periods of ascent, peak patronage, and eventual decline.
2.  **Compare and Contrast:** Place a late work of the artist next to an early work of the emerging paradigm.
3.  **Evaluate Technical/Conceptual Changes:** List the specific innovations the public began to demand that the older artist either rejected or couldn't master.
4.  **Contextualize the Obsolescence:** Understand that obscurity often speaks more to changing societal/cultural tastes than to a decline in the artist's inherent skill.
