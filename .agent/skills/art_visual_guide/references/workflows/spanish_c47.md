# Workflow: Spanish

## Purpose
To evaluate works within Spanish Romanticism, specifically focusing on Francisco Goya's approach to warfare, individual suffering, and the darker side of the human psyche.

## Heuristics
- **Focus on the Individual**: Does the depiction of historical events (e.g., warfare) prioritize individual suffering over collective glory?
- **Exploration of the Psyche**: Does the artwork delve into madness, superstition, the supernatural, or the macabre?
- **Graphic Expression**: How does the medium enhance the exploration of dark themes?

## Steps
1. **Analyze the Subject Matter**: Identify if the work deals with warfare, the supernatural, or psychological distress.
2. **Evaluate the Perspective**: Determine if the narrative perspective emphasizes the victim's experience rather than heroic ideals.
3. **Assess the Technique**: Examine how the artist uses graphic or painterly techniques to convey psychological depth.
4. **Synthesize the Theme**: Conclude how the work reflects the core tenets of Spanish Romanticism.
