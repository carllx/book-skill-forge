# Workflow: Action painting

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow guides the analysis of Action painting, focusing on the physical act of creation as the primary content and emotional expression.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Emphasize the Act**: Analyze how the physical process of painting (pouring, dripping, spattering) supersedes traditional representational goals.
2. **Evaluate Emotional Resonance**: Read the canvas as a record of the artist's raw emotions and physical energy during creation.
3. **Review Technique Innovations**: Consider Hans Hofmann's early experiments and Jackson Pollock's culmination of the style.
4. **Deconstruct Traditional Boundaries**: Acknowledge the abandonment of upright easels and standard brushes in favor of floor-based, immersive canvases.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce an analysis that interprets an action painting primarily through its kinetic energy, material application, and emotional index.
