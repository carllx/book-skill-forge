# Workflow: Analyzing Late Mannerist/Early Baroque Emotionalism (Federico Barocci)

## Purpose
To evaluate works from the transition between Mannerism and the Baroque, focusing on dynamic compositions, soft coloration, and intense emotional warmth, using Federico Barocci as a model.

## Heuristics
- **Color Sfumato**: Look for a pastel-like softness in the colors and a gentle blending of tones (sfumato) that creates a luminous, ethereal effect.
- **Dynamic Asymmetry**: Identify compositions that break away from Renaissance stability, using diagonals and swirling movement.
- **Intimate Devotion**: Focus on the emotional accessibility of the figures. Look for expressions of tender, immediate religious devotion or human connection.

## Execution Steps
1. **Color and Light Audit**: Examine the palette. How do the soft, shimmering colors and delicate lighting contribute to the emotional tone of the piece?
2. **Compositional Flow**: Trace the movement of the figures. How do the sweeping curves and diagonal lines guide the eye through the narrative?
3. **Emotional Resonance**: Evaluate the faces and gestures. Are they stylized (like Mannerism) or do they aim for a direct, empathetic connection with the viewer?
4. **Stylistic Placement**: Synthesize the findings to place the work within its historical context, balancing Mannerist elegance with the approaching drama of the Baroque.
