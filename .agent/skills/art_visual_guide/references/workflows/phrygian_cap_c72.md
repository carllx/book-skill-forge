# Workflow: Layered Symbolism (The Phrygian Cap)

## 1. Core Concept
Analyzing and deploying symbols that contain multiple, sometimes conflicting, layers of historical and contemporary meaning (e.g., a cap that means both "Trojan origin," "freed slave," and "revolutionary liberty").

## 2. Heuristics for Symbolic Deployment
*   **Historical Grounding:** Understand the original or literal meaning of the object within the narrative.
*   **Contemporary Overtones:** Recognize how the current audience's political or social context alters the symbol's reading.
*   **Ironic Contrast:** Exploit the friction between the symbol's meanings (e.g., a symbol of liberty worn by a character about to be enslaved).

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1.  **Identify the Motif:** Select an object, garment, or gesture central to the composition.
2.  **Trace Lineage:** Document its historical origins and traditional iconographic meaning.
3.  **Analyze Contemporary Context:** Determine how recent events (e.g., a revolution) have repurposed the motif.
4.  **Synthesize the Irony/Depth:** Place the motif in a narrative context where its multiple meanings resonate, adding depth or subtle political commentary to the work.
