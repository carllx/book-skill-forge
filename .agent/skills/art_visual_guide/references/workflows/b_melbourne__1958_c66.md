# Workflow: Analyzing Hyperrealistic Sculpture (Ron Mueck)

## Purpose
Use this workflow to analyze hyperrealistic sculptures that play with scale, specifically looking at how artists like Ron Mueck achieve emotional resonance.

## Heuristics
- **Scale Manipulation**: Observe how the work deviates from life-size. Does miniaturization create intimacy, or does monumentalization evoke awe or discomfort?
- **Uncanny Detail**: Look for obsessive detailing (hair, skin texture, blemishes) that pushes the work past realism into the psychological 'uncanny valley.'
- **Narrative Isolation**: Note the emotional state of the figure. Hyperrealistic works often portray isolated, vulnerable, or introspective moments.

## Execution Steps
1. **Initial Scale Assessment**: Compare the sculpture\'s dimensions to an actual human body. Document the immediate psychological effect of this discrepancy.
2. **Detail Audit**: Zoom in on micro-details (e.g., veins, wrinkles). Evaluate how these details ground the work in reality despite the manipulated scale.
3. **Contextual Evaluation**: Analyze the pose and expression. Consider how the figure relates to its surrounding space and the viewer.
4. **Synthesis**: Combine the effects of scale, detail, and emotion to determine the core psychological message of the piece (e.g., mortality, vulnerability).
