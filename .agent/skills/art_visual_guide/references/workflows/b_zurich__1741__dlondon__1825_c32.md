# Workflow: Analyzing Romanticism and the Macabre (Henry Fuseli)

## Purpose
To evaluate artworks that explore the subconscious, nightmares, and the supernatural, characteristics of early Romanticism seen in Henry Fuseli\'s work.

## Heuristics
- **Psychological Tension**: Focus on the depiction of extreme emotional states, fear, or madness.
- **Theatrical Chiaroscuro**: Look for dramatic, stark contrasts between light and dark to isolate figures and heighten drama.
- **Distorted Anatomy**: Observe how human proportions are elongated or contorted to express internal turmoil or supernatural presence.

## Execution Steps
1. **Subject Matter Audit**: Identify supernatural, mythological, or literary themes (e.g., Shakespeare, Milton) that emphasize the irrational or the dark side of human experience.
2. **Lighting Analysis**: Examine how light is used not for realism, but to spotlight psychological terror or divine/demonic forces.
3. **Figurative Distortion**: Analyze the poses and anatomy. Note where physical realism is sacrificed for expressive impact.
4. **Emotional Impact**: Synthesize how the combination of subject, lighting, and distortion creates a sense of the 'sublime' or the uncanny.
