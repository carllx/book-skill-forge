# Workflow: Artistic Movements over Time

## Purpose
To map and analyze the evolution of parallel and diverging artistic schools within a specific historical timeline.

## Heuristics
- **Orthodoxy vs. Innovation**: How do established, orthodox schools contrast with emerging, individualist, or eccentric movements?
- **Simultaneous Development**: Are contrasting schools developing simultaneously, and how do they influence or react to each other?
- **Lineage and Mentorship**: How do artistic principles pass from founders to protégés, and when do they diverge?

## Steps
1. **Establish the Timeline**: Define the historical period.
2. **Identify the Schools**: Map the different groups operating concurrently (e.g., Orthodox, Individualists, Eccentrics).
3. **Analyze the Dynamics**: Compare the conservative approaches of the orthodox groups against the innovative or rebellious methods of the individualists.
4. **Evaluate the Evolution**: Conclude how these parallel movements shaped the overall artistic landscape of the era.
