# Workflow: Evaluating Academic Art & Grand Narratives (Thomas Couture)

## Purpose
To critically analyze 19th-century French academic art, focusing on its reliance on grand historical narratives and the tension between technical virtuosity and perceived sterility.

## Heuristics
- **Theatrical Composition**: Look for staged, highly deliberate arrangements of figures that resemble a theatrical tableau.
- **Allegorical Overload**: Identify historical or mythological subjects used as allegories for contemporary moral or political issues.
- **Technical Rigor vs. Spontaneity**: Assess the tension between highly finished, polished surfaces and underlying structural sketches.

## Execution Steps
1. **Narrative Decoding**: Identify the historical or mythological event depicted. What is the intended moral or allegorical message?
2. **Compositional Mapping**: Trace the arrangement of figures. Look for classical pyramidal structures, balanced staging, and focal points.
3. **Critical Re-evaluation**: Look past the 'academic' label. Are there areas of surprisingly loose brushwork or expressive color that defy the sterile stereotype?
4. **Contextual Reception**: Contrast the artist\'s immense celebrity during their lifetime with their subsequent critical dismissal by modernist movements.
