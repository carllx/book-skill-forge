# Workflow: ALHALKERE, c1910; d ALICE SPRINGS, 1996

## 1. Context and Objective
This workflow analyzes the late-career emergence and profound influence of Indigenous Australian artist Emily Kame Kngwarreye.

## 2. Heuristics & Analysis Steps
1. **Contextualize Isolation & Environment**: Consider how living in central Australia (Alhalkere/Alice Springs) shaped her artistic vision.
2. **Trace Medium Evolution**: Map her progression from batiks (started in 1981) to canvas painting (started in 1989).
3. **Assess Late-Career Impact**: Analyze how beginning formal art production in her 70s influenced her rapid rise (Sydney exhibition 1990, Venice Biennial 1997).
4. **Identify Indigenous Traditions**: Evaluate how traditional Indigenous motifs and connections to the land are synthesized into contemporary abstract forms.

## 3. Output Generation
Produce an evaluation that frames Kngwarreye's work within the intersection of Indigenous Australian heritage and contemporary global art.
