Distinctive Italian cultures can be traced back to the Bronze Age.In the 9th century BCE, the Villanovans flourished briefly in thearea around present-day Tuscany,produing some fine metalwork. In the following century, they were superseded by the Etruscans,an Italian people, whose origins are still hotly disputed. They rapidly builtup an extensive trading network, which brought them considerable wealth.

Italian art played a key role in the history of Western culture. One of the twin pillars of Classicism, Roman art helped provide the basis of European civilization, while the Romans in turn owed much to their Etruscan forebears.

Etruscan artists built on the achievements of the Villanovans, but owed a greater debt to foreign styles. They were heavily influenced by Greek craftsmen, many of whom came to work in Etruria, central Italy. Their artworksalso displayed a strong Near Eastern flavour, which probably stemmed from their trading links with the Phoenicians (natives of present-day Lebanon and Syria).

Roman sculpture served a number of purposes, ranging from pure decoration to political propaganda.
