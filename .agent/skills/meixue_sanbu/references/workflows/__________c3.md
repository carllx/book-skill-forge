# Workflow: Analyzing the Shift from Frontier Vigor to Late-Tang Decadence

## Prerequisites & Context

**When to use this workflow:**
Use this workflow when analyzing the thematic and emotional shift in Tang Dynasty poetry, specifically comparing the robust, patriotic frontier poetry (边塞诗) of the Early and High Tang with the melancholic, decadent poetry of the Late Tang. 

**Why it matters:**
The evolution of Tang poetry is deeply intertwined with the empire's historical trajectory. After the Mid-Tang period, as the dynasty faced internal rebellions (藩镇跋扈), political corruption (宦官窃柄), and external threats, the poetic spirit devolved from heroic ambition to passive despair. Understanding this shift is crucial for contextualizing the "spirit of the age" in literary analysis.

## Comprehensive Guide & Best Practices

### 1. Identify the Spirit of the Early/High Tang (The Frontier Vigor)
When examining poems from this era, look for themes of martial valor, national defense, and personal sacrifice for the greater good.

*   **Key Motifs:** Battlegrounds, armor, horses, archers, and unyielding resolve.
*   **Actionable Insight:** Evaluate how the poet merges personal ambition with national defense.
    > *Example:* "宁知班定远，犹是一书生！" (陈子昂) — Emphasizes that even a scholar harbors the ambition of a great general.
    > *Example:* "功名耻计擒生数，直斩楼兰报国恩！" (张仲素) — Highlights the ultimate sacrifice over mere numerical military achievements.

### 2. Trace the Transition in Tone
Observe how the depiction of the frontier changes. While earlier works focus on glory, some works introduce the harsh realities of war, yet still maintain a heroic undertone.
*   *Example:* "欲将轻骑逐，大雪满弓刀。" (卢纶) — Captures the extreme conditions of the frontier combined with the relentless pursuit of the enemy.

### 3. Analyze the Late-Tang Decadence
When analyzing Late Tang poetry, shift the focus to themes of escapism, helplessness, and the prioritization of personal sorrow over national crises.
*   **Key Motifs:** Romantic longing, physical illness, historical regret, and intricate, ornate imagery (e.g., "鸳鸯蝴蝶", "牡丹").
*   **Actionable Insight:** Contrast the refined rhetoric and strict metrical beauty with the underlying spiritual bankruptcy.
    > *Example:* "下国卧龙空寤主，中原得鹿不由人！" (温庭筠) vs. Du Fu's "出师未捷身先死，长使英雄泪满巾！". The former expresses deterministic helplessness ("不由人"), while the latter evokes profound heroic tragedy.
    > *Example:* "希逸近来成懒病，不能容易向春风。" — Reflects a passive, sickly retreat from reality.

### 4. Evaluate the Ethical vs. Aesthetic Value
A critical best practice in this workflow is to separate aesthetic refinement from ethical fortitude. 
*   Acknowledge the Late Tang poets' mastery of rhetoric, musicality, and ornate language ("修辞学的讲究，字句的美术化").
*   Critique their abandonment of the "poet's character" (诗人的人格) in times of national crisis, where they "只管一己享乐，忘却大众痛苦" (focused only on personal pleasure, forgetting the suffering of the masses).

## If/Then Troubleshooting Logic

*   **IF** the poem contains martial imagery but expresses deep sorrow or anti-war sentiments, **THEN** cross-reference with Du Fu's "non-war literature" (非战文学). Ensure you do not mistake anti-war empathy for Late Tang decadence; the former cares for the masses, while the latter is rooted in self-pity.
*   **IF** a Late Tang poem appears to discuss history or politics (怀古兴感), **THEN** analyze the conclusion. If it resolves into determinism or personal powerlessness ("关张无命欲何如？"), categorize it under Late Tang pessimism rather than High Tang heroic reflection.
*   **IF** you encounter dense historical or biographical references that impede analysis, **THEN** defer to the `graphify_query` MCP tool to unpack the specific historical context before proceeding.

## Verification Checklists

- [ ] **Contextual Alignment:** Have you identified the historical period of the poem and matched it against the empire's state (expansion vs. decline)?
- [ ] **Thematic Extraction:** Are the primary motifs correctly categorized as either "heroic/frontier" or "decadent/escapist"?
- [ ] **Comparative Analysis:** Have you contrasted the poem's attitude towards "merit/nation" (e.g., "立功年", "报国恩") versus "personal sorrow" (e.g., "懒病", "愁")?
- [ ] **Aesthetic vs. Ethical:** Does the analysis acknowledge the technical brilliance of the poetry while critiquing the ideological stance (if analyzing Late Tang)?
