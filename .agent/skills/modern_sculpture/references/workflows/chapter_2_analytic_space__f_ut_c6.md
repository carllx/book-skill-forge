# Workflow: Analyzing Analytic Space in Futurism and Constructivism

## Prerequisites & Context
- **Objective:** Evaluate how an artwork or sculpture dismantles traditional monolithic mass to integrate or structure surrounding space, specifically applying the paradigms of **Futurism** (dynamic interpenetration) and **Constructivism** (structural volume).
- **Core Principle:** Treat space as an active material rather than a passive void. 
- **Knowledge Delegation:** For historical context or deep theoretical grounding on specific manifestos (e.g., Marinetti's *Futurist Manifestos*, Boccioni's *Technical Manifesto*), do not dump theory here. Delegate those inquiries using `graphify_query`.

## Comprehensive Guide & Best Practices

**Step 1: Determine the Primary Spatial Strategy**
Evaluate whether the object prioritizes motion and temporal flux (Futurism) or static, structural articulation of space (Constructivism).

**Step 2: Heuristics for Futurist Analysis (Dynamic Interpenetration)**
- Look for the breakdown of the object's closed boundary. 
- Identify "lines of force" that suggest movement and connect the object to its environment.
> Example: "When analyzing a Futurist piece, track how the contour lines shatter or blur, forcing the viewer to perceive the object and the surrounding atmosphere as a single, continuous field of energy."

**Step 3: Heuristics for Constructivist Analysis (Virtual Volume)**
- Focus on the use of planar elements and industrial materials (e.g., glass, metal, wire) rather than carved or modeled mass.
- Analyze how intersecting planes create "virtual volumes" without filling them with solid matter.
> Example: "Examine a Constructivist structure by tracing its internal voids; the negative space should read as an actively defined volume held together by the tension of the intersecting planes."

## If/Then Troubleshooting Logic

- **If** the artwork presents a closed, uninterrupted surface, **Then** it defies the "Analytic Space" framework. Pivot your analysis to traditional models of mass and monumental sculpture.
- **If** a Futurist piece appears to depict motion but retains a solid, separable boundary, **Then** evaluate it as a representation *of* speed rather than a true spatial interpenetration.
- **If** a Constructivist work uses industrial materials but fills its core with solid mass, **Then** question its adherence to the principle of "volume without mass" and assess whether the materials are merely decorative.
- **If** you lack sufficient theoretical context to explain an artist's intent, **Then** use `graphify_query` to retrieve insights from the relevant primary manifestos.
