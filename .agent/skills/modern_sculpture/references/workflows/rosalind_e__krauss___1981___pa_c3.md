# Workflow: The Terms of Surrealism & Constructivist Vitalism

## Prerequisites & Context
**Why & When to use this workflow:**
This workflow is designed for structural analysis, critique, or generation of forms that balance organic "vitalism" and rationalized geometry. Use it when evaluating sculptural forms, architectural masses, or complex 3D relationships that rely on "truth to material" or seek to evoke a sensation of enclosed volume. 

> **Deep Theory Reference**: For extended theoretical background on Arp's vitalist position, Moore's conceptual tactility, or Giacometti's surrealist time vs. constructivist space, defer to the `graphify_query` MCP tool. 

## Comprehensive Guide & Best Practices

### 1. Form Generation vs. Replication (The Vitalist Heuristic)
Do not replicate given objects; instead, add new forms to the repertory of nature. Form should act as if impregnated with a vital essence that renders it organic.
- **Actionable Step**: Treat your medium not as a blank canvas, but as a biological entity capable of "subdividing" and "fecundating."
- **Reference**: Jean Arp's *Growth* (1938).
> *Example (Arp)*: The sculptor acts as the original Creator, evolving new forms rather than abstracting existing ones.

### 2. "Truth to Material" & The Core-Mass Reciprocity (The Moore Heuristic)
Ensure that the organic development of the material directly influences the organic form of the object.
- **Actionable Step**: Identify the inherent maps within your material (e.g., grain of wood, veining of marble). Let these guide your carving or subtraction process toward the center.
- **Actionable Step**: Establish a contrapuntal relationship between the shape of the hollow core and the resulting exterior mass. The exterior mass should cradle the void "like a vital organ."

### 3. Tactility and Rationalized Volume
Sculpture must be an "art of palpation." A viewer must be able to mentally hold the form, regardless of its scale.
- **Actionable Step**: Visualize your complex form from all around. Identify its center of gravity and weight. Calculate its volume strictly as the space the shape displaces in the air. 

### 4. Constructivist Space vs. Surrealist Time
Differentiate between the temporal and spatial logic of the form.
- **Constructivist/Rationalist (e.g., Moore)**: Position the work in the "pregnant moment of rational sequential, causal time." Form it as if it emerged at intersecting points of a continuous axial grid.
- **Surrealist (e.g., Giacometti)**: Abandon rationalized geometry for an alternate order of perception (psychological time).

## If/Then Troubleshooting Logic

| Condition / Symptom | Diagnosis | Recommended Action |
|---------------------|-----------|--------------------|
| **If** the form feels arbitrarily imposed on the medium... | You have violated "Truth to Material." | **Then** re-examine the material's grain or stratification. Erode the mass guided by its structural geometry rather than an external template. |
| **If** the void appears as an empty hole rather than a vital organ... | There is no reciprocity between container and contained. | **Then** re-shape the hollow core to act as the generative premise of the entire sculptural form. |
| **If** the work feels disjointed when viewed from different angles... | Lacking a "unitary, all-inclusive judgment of depth." | **Then** synthesize the facets so the viewer can mentally grasp the solid shape from a single fixed vantage point. |
| **If** the piece operates in surreal, psychological time rather than geometric space... | You are applying Constructivist rules to a Surrealist object. | **Then** defer to Surrealist heuristics (e.g., *Suspended Ball* logic). Do not attempt to align it with a continuous axial grid. |

## Verification Checklists

### Pre-Execution (Material & Concept)
- [ ] Is the inherent geometry (grain, veins, strata) of the material identified?
- [ ] Is the intention to evolve a new organic form rather than replicate an existing object?
- [ ] Have you mapped the central void or core before defining the exterior boundary?

### Mid-Execution (Process & Tactility)
- [ ] Are the carving tools following the natural striations of the material?
- [ ] Can the solid shape be mentally enclosed "in the hollow of the hand"?
- [ ] Does the exterior mass actively cradle the interior void?

### Post-Execution (Review & Analysis)
- [ ] Does the work exist in a rational, sequential "pregnant moment"?
- [ ] Is the volume accurately perceived as the space it displaces in the air?
