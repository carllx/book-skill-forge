A news conference washeld in Copenhagen and ahalf-hourTV show interviewed some ofthe students. Asa direct consequence of thenewspaperand TV coverage, passage ofa new law seemsprobable in Denmark, makingsuch cabinets (with ergonomically designed latches that arechildsafe) compulsory inall apartmentsand housesrented orsold.

Wehave been able to push thisconcept somewhat further, so that thegovernment of New Zealand is alsoconsidering steps to makesuch a safety cupboard compulsory.
