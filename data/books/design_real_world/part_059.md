Destruction of natural resources may result from apparently benignenvironmental interference. In theearly sixtiesI was involved in the Aswan Dam Building Project. One of the largest structures ofitskind, the Aswan Damproject was specifically designed to provide a multitude of socioeconomic benefits. There would be, minimally, a twenty-five percent increasein cultivated land, andelectrical output would double. Unfortunately, thingsdid not work out like that. Lake Nasser (part of the Aswan development) retains mostof the silton which therich NileDelta farmland depends. Slowly some lessons are being learned. Oneplan that was mercifully never put into practice concerned the Amazon basin.
