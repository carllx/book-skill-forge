Now let usanalyse this as asystem. Distances ofmore than 500milescan be best traversedby airplanes. Distances ofbetween fiftyand500miles are moreefficientlyserved byrailroads, buses, monorail systems and will be better served by other methodsyet to be developed by design teams.

Many devices now exist for traveling distances of less than fifty miles, some of which have not been sufficiently exploited. New ones will be evolved bydesign teams.
