In December 1982, Solar Trek, a solar-powered automobile, was driven from Perth to Sydney, roughly the distance of Philadelphia-San Diego (3500 miles). This experimental vehicle uses a one-horsepower DC electric motorpowered by two normal car batteries. The batteries are constantly charged by 720 photovoltaic cells. The entire trip was trouble free, with theexception offlat tires. (Design World, Australia, No. 1, March 1983.)
