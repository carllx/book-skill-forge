Humidity control inhomesand hospital rooms isimportant and can sometimes become critical. In manyregions of the United States humidity levels aresuch that both humidifiers and dehumidifiers are needed Such gadgetsarecostly, ugly, and ecologically extraordinarily wasteful of water and electricity. Researching this problem fora manufacturer, Robert Senn andI were ableto develop a theoretical humidifier/dehumidifier without moving parts, using noliquids, pumps, or electricity, By combining amix of deliquescentand antibacteriological crystals, wewereabletodevelop a theoretical surface that would store twelve to twenty-four atoms of water toeachcrystal atom and release itagain when humidity was unusually low. Thismaterial could then be sprayed onto a wall or woven into a wallhanging, eliminating the drain on electric power aswell as noise pollution and expense ofpresent-day systems, Experiments have continued for several years, and thedevice now works well. In1982 testmarketing was begun.
