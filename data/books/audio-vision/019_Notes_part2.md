4.  When sound is more and more frequently in long shot, the image tends to differentiate itself from the soundtrack on the level of scale—i.e., there are more closeups. I call this a spontaneous phenomenon because I think it has developed without a great deal of conscious thought. As for the tendency to complementarity: in the combining of auditory long shots and visual closeups, image and sound do not yield an effect of contrast, opposition, and dissonance but rather complementarity. They bring different points of view to the same scene.
