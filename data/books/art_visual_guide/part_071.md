# Albrecht Altdorfer

# REGENSBURG?, c1480; d REGENSBURG 1538

Altdorfer was the leading member of the Danube School, which also included Lucas Cranach and Wolfgang Huber. Although working independently,all these artists used landscape prominently, to adddrama and realism to the stories theytold. Altdorfer's unique contribution was to make pictures that had no story, and were nothing but landscape. In doing so, he instigated a new genre of painting.

One of Altdorfer's early works shows the tiny figure ofSt George, overwhelmed byagreat forest. His celebrated altarpiece (now dismembered) for the Austrian abbey of St Florian featured magnificent skyscapes, and he used dramatic skiesagain to heighten the emotional impactof The Battle of lssus.

As well as painting, Altdorfer produced printsand worked asanarchitect. Although none of his buildings survive,his skill at handling tricky perspective is evident inseveral paintings.
