# Pieter Bruegel the Elder

Pieter Bruegel pioneered the painting ofrural life. With witand colourhe showed peasants hunting, herding, harvesting, and enjoying themselves atfeasts and festivities. Bruegel was nicknamed "The Peasant"but this is misleading, for he was a sophisticated artist anda townsman ratherthan a countryman. Little is known of hisearly years, buthe spent most ofhis working lifein

Likemany northern European painters of this era, Bruegel travelled to Italy, but Italianartseems to have had little impacton him. # Jan van Scorel

# b SCHOORL, NEAR ALKMAAR, 1495; dUTRECHT, 1562

![](images/3d370a2b1128304661f3c5ba0c61e93021ba16207a543da3bf2fb1fd10356fa4.jpg)

Jan van Scorel was one of the first artists to bring the ideas ofthe Italian Renaissance to the area we nowcall Holland. His best paintings show that he grasped the Renaissance sense ofmonumentality, achieved through the use of linear perspective. Furthermore, his figures have a sculptural solidity reminiscentof Andrea Mantegna,while his palette could be as colourful asthe best ofVenetian painting

Portrait by Anthonis Mor

Pope AdrianVI acknowledged Scorel's devotion to Italy- and its classical heritage -byappointing him curator ofthe papal collection ofantiquities, ahighly privileged position previously held by Raphael. Scorel was anamateur archaeologist, engineer, ecclesiastic, and cultivated man of letters aswell asa painter of religious works and portraits. From 1524, he lived in the Low Countries, mainly in Utrecht. His pupils included Marten van Heemskerck and Anthonis Mor.
