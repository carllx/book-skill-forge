# Yves Klein

# b NICE, 1928; d PARIS, 1962

Yves Klein first began to paint while he was a student in the 1940s. Hetravelled widelyin Italy, Britain, Spain,and Japanfrom 1948 to 1952 before settling in Paris in 1955. In 1960, with the critic Pierre Restany, he founded the New Realism movement, which consisted ofa group of artists who rejected traditional easel painting.

Most of Klein's early paintings were monochrome ina variety of colours (one ofhis first acts as an artist wasto 'sign" the brilliant blue sky ofthe French Riviera,calling ithis "firstand biggest monochrome' " butby the late 1950s they were almost all deep blue, a colour heeventuallypatented as International Klein Blue (IKB) although it was never produced commercially.

Aswell as conventional paintings, Klein also used models covered in paintand laid upon or dragged across the canvas as living brushes" a technique he called anthropometry.
